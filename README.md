# Toronto Marine Robotics (TMR) 🌊🤖

Welcome to the official GitHub home of **Toronto Marine Robotics**, a premier student-led autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) design team at the University of Toronto (St. George Campus). 

Our mission is to bridge the gap in U of T's robotics portfolio by designing, manufacturing, and programming cutting-edge marine vehicles to compete at the international **RoboSub** competition.

---

## 🚀 The Mission
While the University of Toronto dominates land and aerospace robotics engineering, marine environments present a unique, wide-open challenge. TMR is a fast-velocity engineering team tackling the brutal constraints of underwater autonomy:
*   **Mechanical:** Hydrodynamic, dual-seal pressure vessels rated for depth, active thermal dissipation, and complex modular internal framing.
*   **Electrical:** Custom high-current power distribution boards (PDBs), isolated signal routing, and ruggedized battery management systems (BMS).
*   **Software:** ROS 2 architecture, sensor fusion (IMU + Doppler Velocity Log), and YOLO/OpenCV-driven deep learning for subterranean vision and acoustic localization.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
We treat software and hardware as deeply integrated systems. Our current engineering roadmap centers on:
*   **Core OS & Middleware:** ROS 2 + LCM, Ubuntu Server / Linux
*   **Embedded & Firmware:** STM32 (C/C++ & Rust), FreeRTOS, KiCad for high-current PCB design
*   **Perception & Autonomy:** Python, C++, YOLO, OpenCV, Issac Lab (for subsea physics simulation)
*   **Edge Computing:** NVIDIA Jetson Orin platform
